# CivicFlow service taxonomy and filters

## Identity, Caste & Revenue Certificates (`identity-certificates`)

**Department filters:** Revenue, General Administration, District Administration

- Caste / Community / SC / ST / OBC certificate
- EWS certificate
- Non-Creamy Layer certificate
- Income certificate
- Domicile / Residence / Nativity certificate
- Age / Nationality certificate
- Senior citizen certificate
- Character certificate
- Legal heir / Family member certificate
- Solvency certificate
- Agriculturist / Small & marginal farmer certificate
- Minority / Backward area / Tribal certificate
- Disability certificate / UDID
- Bonafide resident certificate

## Birth, Death, Marriage & Civil Registration (`birth-death-marriage`)

**Department filters:** Health, Local Body, Planning & Development, Registrar

- Birth registration / birth certificate
- Delayed birth registration
- Birth certificate correction
- Death registration / death certificate
- Delayed death registration
- Marriage registration / certificate
- Marriage certificate correction
- Adoption / family-member related services where applicable

## Land, Property & Revenue (`land-property-revenue`)

**Department filters:** Revenue, Land Records, Registration & Stamps, Survey & Settlement

- Record of Rights / 7/12 / Jamabandi / Patta
- Mutation / Dakhil-Kharij
- Land possession certificate
- Land valuation certificate
- Cadastral map / BhuNaksha
- Encumbrance certificate
- Property registration / deed registration
- Stamp duty / circle rate / market valuation
- Partition / demarcation / survey
- Land-use conversion / non-agricultural permission
- Property tax and municipal assessment

## Ration Card & Food Security (`ration-food`)

**Department filters:** Food & Civil Supplies, Consumer Protection, PDS

- New ration card
- Add / delete family member
- Address change / transfer
- Duplicate ration card
- Ration card correction
- NFSA / PHH / AAY eligibility and status
- Fair-price shop / PDS grievance
- One Nation One Ration Card information

## Pensions, Welfare & Social Security (`pension-welfare`)

**Department filters:** Social Welfare, Women & Child Development, Tribal Welfare, SC/ST Welfare, Labour Welfare

- Old-age pension
- Widow pension
- Disability pension
- Family benefit scheme
- Construction worker registration and benefits
- Maternity assistance
- Scholarship / hostel assistance
- Marriage assistance
- Unemployment assistance
- Senior citizen welfare
- Tribal / SC / ST welfare scheme
- EWS and other targeted assistance

## Education & Scholarships (`education-scholarships`)

**Department filters:** School Education, Higher Education, Technical Education, Social Justice / Tribal Welfare

- Pre-matric scholarship
- Post-matric scholarship
- Merit scholarship
- Fee reimbursement
- Fellowship / research grant
- Hostel application / stipend
- First graduate certificate
- Duplicate marksheet / migration certificate
- Student registration / APAAR / academic records
- State scholarship portal services
- National Scholarship Portal schemes

## Labour, Employment & Worker Registration (`labour-employment`)

**Department filters:** Labour, Employment & Training, Skill Development, Factories & Boilers

- eShram registration/update
- Employment exchange registration/renewal
- Building & construction worker registration
- Labour welfare board benefits
- Shop & Establishment registration
- Factory licence / renewal
- Contract labour licence
- Inter-state migrant worker registration
- Apprenticeship / skill training
- Unemployment allowance / job-seeker services
- EPFO UAN / claims / passbook
- ESIC registration and benefits

## Driving, Vehicles & Transport (`transport-vehicles`)

**Department filters:** Transport, Road Transport Authority, Traffic Police

- Learner driving licence
- Driving licence new / renewal / duplicate
- Address change / class addition in driving licence
- International driving permit
- Vehicle registration / RC
- Ownership transfer / hypothecation
- Vehicle permit / fitness / NOC
- Road tax / eChallan
- Vehicle details and status
- Pollution certificate information
- Public transport permits

## Business, Trade & Professional Licences (`business-licences`)

**Department filters:** Industries, Municipal Administration, Labour, Food Safety, Fire, Pollution Control

- Trade licence
- Shop & Establishment licence
- FSSAI registration/licence
- Factory licence
- Boiler registration
- Fire NOC
- Pollution consent/NOC
- Building plan approval
- Contractor registration
- Professional / trade permits
- Company / society / firm registration
- State and central business approvals via NSWS

## Utilities & Municipal Services (`utilities-municipal`)

**Department filters:** Electricity, Water Supply, Municipal Corporation, Urban Development

- New electricity connection
- Electricity bill payment / correction
- Water connection / bill
- Property tax payment
- Waste / sanitation complaint
- Building permission
- Streetlight / road / drainage grievance
- Trade and occupancy permissions
- Local body certificates and services

## Health, Insurance & Disability (`health-insurance`)

**Department filters:** Health & Family Welfare, Medical Education, Social Justice, Insurance

- Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY eligibility/card
- ABHA Health ID
- UDID disability certificate/card
- Health scheme registration
- Medical reimbursement assistance
- Birth-related health scheme registration
- State health insurance scheme services

## Agriculture, Farmers & Rural Development (`agriculture-rural`)

**Department filters:** Agriculture, Horticulture, Animal Husbandry, Fisheries, Rural Development

- PM-KISAN registration/status/eKYC
- Farmer ID / farmer registration
- Kisan Credit Card guidance
- Crop insurance
- Soil health / soil testing
- Horticulture/agriculture subsidy
- Irrigation and farm equipment subsidy
- Animal husbandry/fisheries support
- MGNREGS job card and payments
- Rural housing and livelihood schemes

## Passport, Voter & Travel Permits (`citizenship-travel`)

**Department filters:** External Affairs, Election Commission, Home / District Administration

- Fresh/reissue passport
- Tatkaal passport
- Police Clearance Certificate
- Voter registration Form 6
- Correction / deletion Form 7 / Form 8
- e-EPIC download
- Polling station and voter-list search
- Inner Line Permit
- Protected/restricted-area permits where applicable

## Grievance, RTI & Appeals (`grievance-rti`)

**Department filters:** Administrative Reforms, Public Grievance, Information Commission, Departmental Appellate Authority

- CPGRAMS grievance
- State grievance / CM helpline
- RTPS service-delivery appeal
- First/second/third appeal under state public service laws
- RTI application to Central Public Authority
- State RTI application (state-specific portal)
- Department grievance and service complaint
- Application status and service-delivery escalation

## Digital Documents & Citizen Accounts (`digital-documents`)

**Department filters:** MeitY / NeGD, UIDAI, DigiLocker Issuers

- Aadhaar download / address update / status
- DigiLocker issued documents
- PAN services (official Income Tax portal)
- DigiLocker fetch/share/verify
- State certificate verification/download
- Digital signature / eSign where supported

## Tourism, Culture & Local Permits (`tourism-local`)

**Department filters:** Tourism, Culture, District Administration, Forest

- Tourist guide / operator recognition
- Hotel registration / classification
- Protected area / entry permit
- Museum or monument e-ticketing
- Film/event permissions
- Forest produce and local permits

## Recommended filters

- State/UT
- District → Tehsil/Taluka/Block/Circle (when service requires it)
- Department (dynamic based on state)
- Category
- Search + aliases + local-language names
- Application mode: online / CSC-kiosk / offline / hybrid
- Government availability: available / temporarily unavailable / offline only / unknown
- CivicFlow integration: directory only / external link / browser-stream candidate / browser-stream tested / native integration
- Documents: official checklist verified / missing / updated date
- Popular / recently added / recently verified
- Language
